Before you ask

Is the skipper included?

Yes. Melkart I is only chartered with a professional skipper, and the skipper is included in the price.

What does the day charter include?

The 2,500 € day price covers the skipper, fuel, cleaning and base-port fees. Up to 10 guests plus the skipper.

Why can't I book in October or May?

Those are the months she crosses the Atlantic: to Nassau in October and back to Mallorca in May. The calendar stays blocked in case of delays at sea.

How many people can sleep aboard?

Ten guests in five cabins: four doubles and one twin. The skipper has a separate crew cabin.

Which toys are on board?

A 3.8 m Zar Mini tender with a 20 hp outboard, two paddle boards, snorkel sets and a floating, jellyfish-proof sea pool.
